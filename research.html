Research

Topological and Geometrical Deep Learning

The main objective of this subgroup is to study neural networks by means of topological and geometrical methods. We focus on both the theoretical analysis of neural networks and applications to computer vision problems. We are associated with the Topological Machine Learning group at the Universitat de Barcelona, and we coordinate the reading group in theoretical deep learning.

Members: Manuel Lecha, Rubén Ballester

Projects:

  • Topological Data Analysis for Neural Network Analysis.
  • Generative models of neural networks.

Associated publications:

  • Preprint: Predicting the generalization gap in neural networks using topological data analysis
